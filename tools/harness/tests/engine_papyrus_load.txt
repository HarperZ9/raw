# Papyrus under load: AI on, in the Bannered Mare and in Riverwood. The opcode loop is timed at its call site;
# each scene is measured with the game's budget, then adaptive, then fixed at 8 ms.
start load rawharness
scene inn

scene riverwood
console coc riverwood
wait 10
engine measure rw_game 900
engine papyrus adaptive 16.667 0.5 8; wait 60; measure rw_adaptive 900
engine papyrus off
quit
