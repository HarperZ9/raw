# A vista over Riverwood for LOD and grid proofs: the player floats (tcl) 12,000 units up and looks along
# the valley. The moves go in one bat file: after a separate 'tcl' the next console line went into the
# world twice (P opened the magic menu), with and without a close key after it. Run once with the defaults and once with [EngineSet] LOD and grid values; compare the captures.
start load rawharness
console tai
scene vista
console set gamehour to 13
console coc whiterunbanneredmare
wait 8
console coc riverwood
wait 8
console fw 81a
wait 4
bat tcl|player.setpos z 12000|player.setangle x 15
wait 15
engine measure vista 600; capture vista
bat player.setangle z 180
wait 10
engine measure vista_back 600; capture vista_back
quit
